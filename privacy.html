Privacy policy

Tilawah: Quran Reader. Last updated 3 October 2026.

Tilawah is a free app for reading and listening to the Quran, prayer times and daily remembrance. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. This policy explains what happens to information while you use it.

Information kept on your device

Your reading place, bookmarks, reading and dhikr statistics, athkar progress, settings and any translations or recitations you download (including the location you choose for prayer times) are saved only in your device's app storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes them.

Location

If you choose "Use my current location", the app asks your device for your position once and uses it on your device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Your location is not sent anywhere. You can instead pick a city or enter coordinates, and you can turn off location access in your device settings at any time.

Motion and compass

The Qibla compass reads your device's orientation sensors while the compass is on. This data is used only on your device and is never stored or sent.

Services the app connects to

To keep the app small, some content is downloaded when you first use it:

  • Quran translations from the open quran-api dataset, delivered by jsDelivr and GitHub.
  • Recitation audio from EveryAyah.com when you use listening mode, and from Hugging Face (the Maqra archive) when you download recitations for offline listening.

As with any internet request, these services receive your device's IP address and basic technical details (such as browser type) so they can deliver the files. The app sends them nothing else. Their own privacy policies apply to how they handle this.

Children

Tilawah is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
